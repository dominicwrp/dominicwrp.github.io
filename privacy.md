# Sole Garden privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Sole Garden is a free walking game: you walk, and your plants grow. It is made by Terence and Dominic in Singapore. This page says what the game keeps about you, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted. We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Questions, or to ask for your data or its deletion: **dominicwrp@gmail.com**

## The short version

- The game reads one kind of health data: your step count. It uses it to grow your plants and set your daily goal. Your steps are never used for ads or marketing, never sold, and never given to an ad network.
- There is no sign-up. The game makes an account with no email and no real name. You pick a display name only if you use Friends.
- We never sell or share your data. The only outside services are the ones that run the game: our server, notifications, and Google's ads.
- Settings, Delete my account removes your account and what is stored about you on our server.

## Steps (Apple Health and Health Connect)

- On iPhone the game reads your step count from Apple Health. On Android it reads your step count from Health Connect. It asks for steps only, and only to read. It never writes to them and never reads anything else (no heart rate, sleep, weight or location).
- What it does with them: grows your plants, sets your daily goal from your own walking, and shows your week's steps.
- What reaches our server: each day's step total and goal, whether you reached your goal, and how much you played. We use these numbers only to see whether the game helps people walk and whether the daily goal is set right.
- Your friends see whether you reached your goal. They see your step numbers only if you turn that on (Settings, Privacy), and you can hide them from chosen friends.
- Steps are never used for ads or marketing, never sent to the ad network or to any analytics company, and never sold.
- On Android, Sole Garden's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## What the game keeps, and why

**On your phone:** your whole game (your plants, honey and everything you own), your settings, and, if you give it, your year of birth. Your year of birth stays on the phone and is never sent anywhere.

**On our server**, so friends and shared gardens work and your game is kept safe:

- **Your account:** a random ID made by the game, your friend code, your display name, your pet, a picture of your garden as friends see it, and your phone's time zone (so notifications keep to daytime where you are).
- **Your game, by day:** how many times you opened the game and for how long, your steps and goal (see above), the honey you earned and spent, the arcade games you played, and the game's version.
- **Friends and gardens:** your friends, friend requests and blocks; what you send friends (messages, nudges, seeds, cuttings, admires); your community gardens, their chat and reactions, and what you gave or built there; arcade scores and challenges.
- **Photos you choose:** a photo pet. It is checked on your phone before it is sent, and friends see it in your garden.
- **Notifications:** your phone's notification address, so friends' actions can reach you.
- **Ads:** when you choose to watch an ad, which screen it was on and whether it finished. Never your steps or your year of birth.
- **Reports:** if you report a player, a message or an ad, the report and its reason, so we can act on it.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.

## Ads

- Ads in Sole Garden are always your choice: you tap to watch one for honey. There are no ads you did not ask for.
- The ads come from Google AdMob. Google may use your phone's advertising ID and basic device information to show and count ads, as its policy says: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Children:** the game asks for your year of birth before your first ad. Anyone under 13, or anyone who does not answer, gets ads marked for children, rated G and never personalised, and is never asked about tracking.
- **Everyone else:** where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks you first. On iPhone, Apple's tracking question asks too. If you say no, the ads are not personalised. Saying no changes nothing in the game.

## Who else handles your data

Only the services that run the game, each for that job alone:

- **Supabase**, our server, in Singapore: your account and everything listed above.
- **Apple Push Notification service and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging**, through **Expo**: to deliver notifications to your phone.
- **Expo**: to deliver game updates to the app.
- **Google AdMob**: the ads you choose to watch.

We never sell your data, and we never share it with anyone for their own use.

## Children

Sole Garden is for children and adults. It needs no email or real name. Children's ads are marked for children and never personalised, and their year of birth stays on the phone. Friends' step numbers are hidden unless the player turns them on. A parent can ask us to delete a child's data at the email above.

## How long we keep it, and deleting it

- We keep your data while your account exists.
- **Settings, Delete my account** deletes your account and everything stored about you on our server: your game, friends, messages, photos and daily numbers. It cannot be undone.
- Reports that keep players safe may be kept after an account is deleted.
- You can also write to us to see what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. We answer within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the app removes what is on your phone, but not your account on our server. Use Delete my account first.

## Keeping it safe

Your data travels encrypted. On our server, each player can reach only their own data, and what friends see goes through checks first. Only Terence and Dominic can reach the game's admin pages, and every admin action is checked and logged.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with its date. A big change is also said in the game.
